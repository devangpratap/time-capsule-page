Time Capsule

Write a letter to your future self. It is encrypted on this device before it goes anywhere, and emailed to you on the date you pick. Until then it is stored only in encrypted form.

Use an email address you'll still have in five years.

Now hand it in

  1. Copy the sealed letter below.
  2. Open the hand-in page (you need to be signed in to GitHub), paste it, and press Create.
  3. Within a minute you'll get a confirmation email. That means it's stored safely.
